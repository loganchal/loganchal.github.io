# Academic profile and hover imagery

The profile uses a conventional portrait-and-links sidebar with biography and two subject panels. Oceanography precedes Mathematics; the model explorer remains a direct link to the independently deployed project.

Oceanography cycles seven distinct Monet coastal paintings while hovered or focused. Mathematics cycles four nature-dominated landscapes by Corot, Claude Lorrain and Duncanson. Images decode before crossfading, rotation stops when the panel is no longer engaged, and reduced motion disables timed cycling. Painting images load near the profile rather than with the opening scene. Poplars at Giverny (1887, Museum Barberini) is reserved for the tree paper. Sources and full reproductions are at /paintings/ and in asset manifests; no citation overlays appear on the panels.

Validation: Chrome desktop 1512×982 and widths 834, 390, 320; no horizontal overflow or JavaScript errors. All seven Oceanography paintings visited without a repeat; four Mathematics images visited without a repeat. Reduced-motion hover remained stable beyond the timer interval.
